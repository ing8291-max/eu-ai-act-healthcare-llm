# W1D2 — 2026-09-01

## What I read
Art. 3, definitions 1–4: `AI system`, `risk`, `provider`, `deployer`.

## What I learned

**1. `risk` is a combination, and the Act does not say how to combine.**
Art. 3(2): "the combination of the probability of an occurrence of harm
and the severity of that harm." It does not prescribe multiplication or
any other formula. Low-probability/high-severity and
high-probability/low-severity are not equated by the text.

The method of combination appears to be left to the provider — which
would mean it has to be chosen and documented. To verify against Art. 9
in W3.

Note: I first assumed this meant probability × severity. It doesn't say
that. Corrected.

**2. `provider` covers both systems and models.**
Art. 3(3) explicitly says "an AI system **or** a general-purpose AI
model". So the LLM vendor and I are both providers; only the object
differs.

| Actor | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Me | Provider of an AI system 
| Hospital | Deployer | 
| LLM vendor | Provider of a GPAI model |

**3. "High-risk" is not defined in Art. 3.**
Reading definitions 1–4, I expected `high-risk` to appear near `risk`.
It does not. Section 1 of Ch. III is titled "Classification of AI
systems as high-risk", which suggests it is determined by a procedure
rather than stated as a definition. To confirm in W2 by reading Art. 6.

## What confused me
Roles (Art. 3) and obligations (Ch. III) are separate layers. Being a
`provider` does not by itself mean Ch. III applies to me. Ch. III covers
high-risk AI systems — and whether my system counts as high-risk is
decided somewhere in Art. 6, which I have not read yet.
